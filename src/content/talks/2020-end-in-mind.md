In the [Catalyst Center’s][catalyst] SBIR & STTR Summit 2020, I applied one of the [Seven Habits of Highly Effective People][habits] to developing a [SBIR or STTR][sbir] Phase&nbsp;I proposal, namely beginning with the end in mind to walk evaluators through a clear path of the Phase&nbsp;I feasibility study, the Phase&nbsp;II prototype, and Phase&nbsp;III commercialization.

* Benefits to Small Business and the Government
* What the SBIR Program Is Not
* Purpose of the SBIR Program
* Commercialization Strategy
* Make It a Sales Document!
