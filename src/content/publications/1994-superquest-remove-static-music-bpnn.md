Our team from the [Alabama School of Mathematics and Science][asms] was one of multiple winners who got to attend the SuperQuest Summer Institute at The [University of Alabama in Huntsville][uah], where we worked with where we worked with Dr.&nbsp;John Ziebarth, his colleagues at the UAH [Computer Science Department][uahcs], and scientists at NASA’s Marshall Space Flight Center. Our project used a back-propagation neural network to remove static from music. We optimized our software on UAH infrastructure and using resources at the [Alabama Supercomputer Authority][asc].
