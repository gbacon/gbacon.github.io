Back in 1995, an acquaintance who worked at EDS pointed me to an interesting website where visitors were presented with a simple form. You could type in a few words, and away it would go, searching the entire web for pages containing those words. These days, search engines are commonplace, but AltaVista represented a cool new idea back then and was an interesting way for DEC to showcase its new Alpha processor.

Although cool, AltaVista wasn’t perfect. A few years later, Google attempted to solve the relevance problem with PageRank. Google interprets a link from page A to page B as a vote by page A for page B, weighing votes from important pages more heavily. Google has limitations, but it was uncanny how frequently its results were right on.
